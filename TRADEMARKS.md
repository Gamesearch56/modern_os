# The ModernOS name and logo

The code of ModernOS is free software under the GNU GPL version 3 or later (see [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [NOTICE](NOTICE)). The **name** "ModernOS" and the **ModernOS logo** are not part of that license. They identify the project maintained by Gamesearch56, so people know what they are getting.

## You may, without asking

- Use, study, and change ModernOS for yourself, at home or at work.
- Share unchanged copies of the official releases under the name ModernOS.
- Write about ModernOS, review it, make videos and tutorials, and link to it.
- Say that your project is "based on ModernOS" or "a fork of ModernOS", as long as it has its own name.
- Contribute changes back to this repository.

## You may not, without written permission

- Distribute a modified version under the name ModernOS, or under a name or logo that is easy to confuse with it.
- Present a modified version, or ModernOS itself, as your own work or as an official release.
- Suggest that Gamesearch56 or ModernOS endorses your product, service, or fork.
- Remove the attribution described in [NOTICE](NOTICE).

## If you fork

Pick a new name and a new logo, keep the NOTICE file and the "Based on ModernOS by Gamesearch56" credit, and say clearly that your version is changed. Your fork stays under the GPL, so its source stays open for everyone, just like this one.

Questions or permission requests: open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/Gamesearch56/modern_os/issues).
